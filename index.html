The game

Build your town

Houses, fields, lumber camps, quarries and markets. Your villagers chop, dig, farm and build, and every building can grow from a timber hut to stone.

Hold the line

When night falls the raiders come. Raise walls and gates, man your towers with archers and crossbowmen, and rally your soldiers where the fight is hardest.

Control siege engines

Ballistas, catapults and trebuchets, worked by your own villagers. Wheel them into place and break the raiders' rams and engines before they reach the walls.

Earn and upgrade

Win stars on every stage and spend them on upgrades for your town, your defences and your castle. Then try each stage again on Hard for its crown.
