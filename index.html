The game

Build by day

Put your villagers to work on wood, stone and food. Timber huts become stone halls as the town grows.

Hold by night

Raiders arrive at dusk. Meet them with walls, archers and soldiers you can rally to wherever the line is thinnest.

Bring siege engines

Ballistas, catapults and trebuchets, crewed by your own villagers. The raiders bring theirs too.

Spend your stars

Each stage awards up to 3 stars. Spend them on 36 permanent upgrades, then return on Hard for a crown.
