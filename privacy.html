Privacy Policy

Castle Defense: Medieval · last updated 13 November 2026

In short: Castle Defense: Medieval does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not use your camera or microphone. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and no third-party code that collects data.

Your game progress

Your stars, upgrades, achievements, settings and saved game are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The full game is sold as an in-app purchase through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account. The game only asks the App Store whether the full game has been bought on your account, so it can unlock it. Apple's handling of your information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Internet use

The game plays fully offline. It only goes online to talk to the App Store when you buy or restore the full game.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@castledefense.app.
