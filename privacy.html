Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Summary

  • No personal data collected
  • No accounts, ads or analytics
  • Progress stays on your device
  • Purchases handled by Apple

Data

The game does not ask for or access your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

Progress

Stars, upgrades, achievements, settings and saved games are stored on your device and are not transmitted. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The full game is an in-app purchase processed by Apple. The developer receives no payment details and no information about your Apple Account. The game asks the App Store only whether the purchase exists, in order to unlock it. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Network

The game runs offline. It contacts the App Store only when you buy or restore the full game.

Children

No personal data is collected from any player, including children.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published here with a new date.
