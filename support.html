Support

Write to support@castledefense.app. Include your device, the stage you were on and what happened. A screenshot helps.

How do I restore my purchase?

In the game, open Settings from the title screen and tap Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. It survives updates. Deleting the app erases it, though your purchase can always be restored.

How do I start the campaign over?

Settings, then Reset campaign. Tap twice to confirm. Stars, upgrades and achievements are cleared. Your purchase is not.

The game runs slowly or the device gets warm

Set Graphics to Low in Settings or in the pause menu.

There is no sound

Check the Music and Sounds sliders in Settings, then the device volume. The silent switch does not mute the game.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only to buy or restore the full game.

What does the full game add?

Stages 4 to 12, both boss battles, three bonus stages and four more colours. Stages 1 to 3 are free.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
