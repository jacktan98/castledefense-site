Support

Castle Defense: Medieval for iPhone and iPad

Found a bug or need help? Email support@castledefense.app. Please tell us your device (for example iPhone 16 or iPad Air), which stage you were on, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Common questions

I bought the full game. How do I get it on another device, or after reinstalling?

Open the game, tap the gear on the title screen to open Settings, then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Your stars, upgrades and saved game stay there when you close or update the game, but deleting the app deletes them. Your purchase is kept by Apple and can always be restored.

How do I start the campaign again?

Settings, then Reset campaign, and tap it a second time to confirm. This clears your stars, upgrades and achievements. It does not remove your purchase.

The game runs slowly or my phone gets warm.

Set Graphics to Low in Settings, or in the pause menu during a game. It draws fewer pixels and turns off shadows and most of the weather.

I hear no music or sound.

Check the Music and Sounds sliders in Settings and your device's volume. The game plays sound even with the silent switch on.

Do I need the internet to play?

No. The whole game plays offline. You only need a connection to buy or restore the full game.

What is in the free version?

Stages 1 to 3 are free. One purchase unlocks Stages 4 to 12, the boss battles, the three bonus stages and four more town colours.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
